NEED TO KNOW MORE?

Frequently asked questions.

Here are some answers to help you get started.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Our websites are designed to adapt to smartphones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can I connect my own domain?

Yes. We can help connect a domain you own. Domain registration costs are separate.

Can you integrate a checkout platform?

Yes. Checkout integration is available in the Premium package, subject to platform compatibility and access.

Can I request changes to my website?

Revision allowances depend on the package selected. Additional changes can be discussed separately.

What do you need to start my project?

We need your project brief, content, brand assets and any relevant links or references.
